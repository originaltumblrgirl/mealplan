Your ADHD food operating system
Remove decisions. Anchor to habits. Keep components, not full meals.
1
Anchor meals, not menus. Breakfast is locked — egg rice bowl every day. For lunch, rotate between 3 options max. Dinner is your partner's domain. No decisions required.
2
Component-based eating. Buy cucumbers, hummus, noodles, rice, tofu, greens weekly. These combine into 8+ meals without real cooking. The meal assembles itself.
3
The 2-minute lunch rule. When stressed, options must be under 2 min prep. Mini cukes + hummus, noodle pack, sourdough sandwich — all qualify. Pre-slice turkey and pre-wash greens on Sunday.
4
Visual pantry = used pantry. Hummus, cucumbers, olives, avocados go at eye level in the fridge. If you have to move things to find food, it won't happen when dysregulated.
5
Brain-dead backup. Always stock 2–3 Momofuku packs + canned white beans + jarred salsa. When nothing sounds good and you can't think — that's dinner, no shame.
6
Sunday 15-min prep. Wash and bag greens. Slice turkey and stack in deli drawer. Cook a big pot of rice. Boil 6 eggs. That's it — the week is handled.

Key Ayurvedic nudges
Eat at consistent times. Vata is regulated by routine. Your egg rice breakfast every morning is already Ayurvedic medicine.

Warm over cold when stressed. A hot soup or warm rice bowl calms Vata. Cold salads on a stressful day can increase anxiety and scatter.

Never skip meals. Non-negotiable for Pitta. Blood sugar crashes hit Pitta types hard and spiral into irritability and brain fog.

Eat away from screens when possible. Even once a week. Pitta digestion is strong, but stress eating overrides it. Five minutes away from the desk counts.
